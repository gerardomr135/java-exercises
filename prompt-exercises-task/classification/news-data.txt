Harry Potter movies became a global hit – Starting in 2001, the first Harry Potter movie was released in London, UK, and soon kids all over the world were reading the books and watching the films, making it one of the most popular stories ever.

Apple released the iPhone – In 2007 in California, USA, Apple launched the first iPhone. It changed how people use phones, letting them browse the internet, play games, and take photos all in one device.

The Deepwater Horizon oil spill – In 2010, an oil rig exploded in the Gulf of Mexico, USA, causing one of the largest oil spills in history. Thousands of animals were affected, and the ocean was seriously polluted.

The rise of social media – Platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram grew hugely around 2006–2015, helping people all over the world share pictures, videos, and messages in real time.

The Rio 2016 Olympics – In 2016, athletes from all over the world competed in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, showing incredible sports skills and inspiring millions of kids to play sports.

The Paris Climate Agreement – In 2015, world leaders met in Paris, France, and agreed to take action against climate change, promising to protect the Earth from global warming.

The rise of K-pop worldwide – Around 2018–2020, K-pop groups like BTS became super popular everywhere, from South Korea to the USA, influencing music, fashion, and culture.

The election of Pope Francis – In 2013, Pope Francis became the leader of the Catholic Church in Vatican City. He became famous for being kind, simple, and caring about the poor.

Usain Bolt’s Olympic victories – In 2008 and 2012, Usain Bolt from Jamaica became the fastest man in the world at the Olympics, breaking records and inspiring young athletes worldwide.

The solar and wind energy boom – Over the 2010s, countries like Germany, China, and the USA built huge numbers of solar panels and wind turbines to make clean energy, helping fight pollution and climate change.
